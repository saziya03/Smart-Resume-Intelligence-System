Job Title: NLP Engineer

We are looking for an NLP Engineer with experience in Python, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing to build intelligent language-based solutions. The candidate will develop, fine-tune, and deploy NLP models for tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, information extraction, and conversational AI.

Key Skills:

Python
NLP (NLTK, spaCy, Hugging Face)
Machine Learning & Deep Learning
Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch
Transformers (BERT, GPT, etc.)
Text Processing & Feature Engineering
SQL and Data Handling

Responsibilities:

Develop and optimize NLP models and pipelines.
Fine-tune transformer-based models.
Build and deploy AI-powered applications.
Evaluate and improve model performance.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver NLP solutions.

Preferred:

Experience with LLMs, RAG, ChromaDB/FAISS, and Vector Embeddings.
Knowledge of model deployment and MLOps.
